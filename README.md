# Song_Popularity_Trend_Analysis_2017
In this project, Spotify streaming trends for top songs of 2017 were analyzed using Python with Pandas and Matplotlib. Time series analysis revealed differences in release performance, audience engagment, and evolving music consumption patterns over the year.

![Daily Spotify Streams - Top Songs in 2017](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6b91a8b5-3cd2-43d9-af29-d6a2cbacbdb0)

**Interpretation:**

**Shape of You**

-Trend: Peaked early in the year after release, followed by a steady, gradual decline with consistently high daily streams throughout 2017.

-Interpretation: This indicates strong launch momentum and long-term listener retention, liklely due to broad appeal and early promotion.

**Despacito**

-Trend: Released later in the year with a rapid incline to the highest single-day streams, followed by a slower, prolonged decline and a late-year spike.

-Interpretation: Reflects viral, global popularity and cultural impact; the late spike may be tied to seasonal events or renewed interests.

**Something Just Like This**

-Trend: Moderate and steady performance without dramatic peaks or drops.

-Interpretation: Suggests consistent, mid-level popularity and long-term engagement without reliance on virality.

**HUMBLE**

-Trend: Sharp spike in streams upon release with a quicker decline over the following weeks.

-Interpretation: Points to strong anticipation and a dedicated fanbase, but with shorter mainstream staying power compared to other hits. 

**Unforgettable**

-Trend: Gradual increase in streams mid-year, and steady streams in the later months.

-Interpretation: Demonstrates a "slow-burn" effect -- gaining popularity over time through word-of-mouth, playlist features, or radio play.
